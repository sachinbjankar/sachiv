Delete your Sachiv account

Sachiv (in.sachiv.app) · Developer: Sachin Jankar

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sachiv and log in.
  2. Committee members: open the सदस्य (Members) tab. Residents: open the माझे (Me) tab.
  3. Tap खाते हटवा (Delete account), enter your password, and confirm.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email sachin.bjankar@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Sachiv account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password) and your name.
  • Your membership in every society.

What is kept

Maintenance payment entries, notices and minutes are part of the housing society's own accounts, so they remain with the society after your personal account is deleted. A society's committee can ask us to delete all of the society's data; we do so within 30 days.
