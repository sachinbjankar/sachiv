Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · App: Sachiv (package in.sachiv.app) · Developer: Sachin Jankar, Pune, India · Contact: sachin.bjankar@gmail.com

Sachiv helps housing society committees keep maintenance accounts, publish notices and minutes, and handle complaints, and lets residents see their own dues. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and how you can delete it.

Information we store

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Society details entered by the committee: society name, address, registration number, monthly maintenance amount and the society's UPI ID.
  • Flat list entered by the committee: flat numbers, owner names, phone numbers and any opening balance.
  • Membership: which society and flat you belong to, and whether you are a committee member or resident.
  • Maintenance records: amounts, dates, payment mode and reference numbers (such as a UPI transaction number) recorded by the committee or reported by a resident.
  • Notices, minutes and complaints written in the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or rent any data.

Payments

Sachiv does not process money. When a resident chooses to pay, the app opens the resident's own UPI app (such as Google Pay or PhonePe) with the society's UPI ID filled in, and the payment happens entirely inside that UPI app. We never see bank, card or UPI PIN details.

Who can see what

  • Committee members of a society can see that society's flat list, members, maintenance records, notices and complaints.
  • A resident can see only their own flat's dues and payment history, the society's notices, and their own complaints.
  • No society can see another society's data. This is enforced by security rules on the server.
  • The developer can see each society's name, flat count and subscription status to manage billing, and may access data only to provide support or fix problems.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a service of Google LLC, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. See Firebase privacy and security.

Sharing

When a committee member uses the app to send a WhatsApp reminder or share a notice image, the app hands that content to WhatsApp or another app the user chooses. Otherwise we share data only if required by law.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Account (खाते) → Delete account (खाते हटवा). This permanently deletes your login and your membership in every society. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.

Maintenance records and notices belong to the housing society's accounts, so they stay with the society after an individual account is deleted, unless the committee removes them. A committee that stops using Sachiv can email us to delete all of the society's data; we do so within 30 days.

Children

Sachiv is meant for adult members of housing societies and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
